A

The Surveyor's Field Book

Agent work is territory. Quoli is the survey sheet: known points, measured lines, and deliberately blank ground. The interface feels like a working field book, not a dashboard.

Lead proposal

Palette

#E7E2D6 #1C2420 #C24A2D #2E6F6B #8B927C

Stone paper, graphite ink, vermilion survey marks, water teal for derived links, olive for contour lines. No gradients; depth comes from rules and tone.

Type

Red Hat Display / Red Hat Text
JetBrains Mono for coordinates, stamps and provenance.
Display 5.5em / 0.94 / -0.058em. Body 0.92em / 1.55.

Density & motion

Compact field-book density: ruled index, open reading page, narrow instrument rail. One authored motion: contour lines draw once when a survey opens; a bearing line connects a mark to its source. Reduced motion removes both.

Why it fits

The product is a record of what was measured, by which instrument, and what remains unknown. A survey sheet gives gaps a native visual language: hatched blank ground instead of a warning card. It is distinctive without inventing capability.

Honest risk

The metaphor can overpower a utility. The paper world must stay disciplined, readable in dark mode, and fast at 390px. If the survey language appears in every label, it becomes costume; keep it in structure and evidence marks, not error copy.

B

The Phosphor Terminal

Quoli is a recorder you read like a terminal transcript. The session is the primary object; every derived view is a command output. It refuses the dashboard and the friendly SaaS hero.

Alternate

Palette

#050806 #6BFF9A #1E6B3A #FFB86B #E6FFF0

Near-black glass, P1 phosphor green, dim green for history, amber for unknowns. One surface, no cards; hierarchy from indentation, case and rules.

Type

JetBrains Mono
One face, 0.7–4.3em. Headline 4.3em / 1.02 / -0.065em.
Body 0.86em / 1.75. No second typeface.

Density & motion

Dense transcript density with generous line-height. One authored motion: the cursor breathes and a line lands with a short bloom; scanlines are static and low-contrast. Reduced motion removes the blink and bloom.

Why it fits

Developers already read agent work in terminals. The transcript is the primary record, so provenance and raw evidence are native instead of hidden in an inspector. It is the most direct answer to "show me what happened."

Honest risk

Terminal cosplay is a real trap. Long review documents, settings and onboarding need readable typography and real focus states, not a command line for everything. If every action becomes a typed command, the product becomes hostile.

C

The One-Bit Desktop

Quoli is a desktop for agent evidence: windows, folders and a trash can, rendered in one bit. The interface is a place, not a page. It refuses the responsive card grid and the SaaS dashboard.

Alternate

Palette

#FFFFFF #000000 50% dither

One bit: black, white and ordered dither. No accent color. Selection inverts; warnings use a drawn triangle; state is carried by pattern, position and text.

Type

Nimbus Sans Narrow
Chicago-style bitmap caps for title bars; Geneva-style labels for body.
Display 4.9em / 0.92 / -0.045em. Labels 0.62–0.78em.

Density & motion

Desktop density: overlapping windows, 1px chrome, compact labels. One authored motion: marching ants around the selected session, and a window zoom from folder to document. Reduced motion makes the selection a static dotted outline.

Why it fits

A workspace is a place. Windows are a natural container for sessions, evidence and gaps, and the one-bit constraint forces hierarchy and clarity. It is the most differentiated of the three and the easiest to recognise after one viewport.

Honest risk

Novelty can become costume. Real windows need real focus, drag and z-order, or a fixed tiled layout that only looks like windows. Mobile, keyboard navigation and long review documents are the hard parts; the desktop metaphor must not become a puzzle.
